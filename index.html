Privacy Policy - Taste Spin

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Taste Spin ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information, including information related to the optional Taste Spin PLUS subscription.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: If you choose to sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may process your account identifier, name, email address, and basic profile information necessary for authentication.
  • Saved Content and Preferences: The App may store saved recipes, lists, cooking preferences, and related information locally on your device or through Firebase services when synchronization is available.
  • Device and Diagnostic Information: Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics may collect app usage events, device information, operating system version, app version, diagnostic identifiers, and crash reports to help improve performance and stability.
  • Subscription Information: When you purchase or restore Taste Spin PLUS, the App receives purchase-related information from Google Play Billing, including product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase tokens, as needed to verify subscription access. Subscription purchase and revenue information may also appear in aggregated Google Analytics reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide App functionality and optional account sign-in.
  • To save and synchronize supported user content.
  • To determine whether Taste Spin PLUS is active and unlock subscription features.
  • To restore purchases and troubleshoot subscription issues.
  • To understand App usage and improve user experience.
  • To identify and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements and help prevent misuse.

3. Taste Spin PLUS Subscriptions and Payments

Taste Spin offers an optional auto-renewing subscription called Taste Spin PLUS. The subscription unlocks additional features described within the App. Basic App functionality remains available without purchasing a subscription.

All subscription payments are processed by Google Play. Taste Spin does not directly collect or store your full payment card number, card security code, or complete payment details. Google processes payment information according to its own privacy and payment policies.

Subscription prices, billing frequency, and any available promotional offers are displayed before the purchase is confirmed. Subscriptions renew automatically according to the terms presented by Google Play unless canceled.

You can manage or cancel your subscription through the App's settings or directly through Google Play subscription management . Uninstalling the App does not automatically cancel a subscription.

After cancellation, subscription benefits generally remain available until the end of the paid billing period, subject to Google Play policies and applicable law. Refunds are handled according to applicable Google Play terms and legal requirements.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Google services:

  • Firebase Authentication — optional user authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore — storage and synchronization of supported App data.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — App usage and purchase analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting and diagnostics.
  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases and purchase restoration.

These services may process information according to their respective terms and privacy policies. For additional details, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data Storage and Security

Depending on the feature, information may be stored locally on your device or processed and stored using Firebase and other Google services.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may use the App's basic features without purchasing Taste Spin PLUS.

You may manage or cancel an active subscription using Google Play. You may also contact us to request information about personal data under our control, request deletion where applicable, or exercise other privacy rights available under applicable law.

Some transaction-related records may be retained by Google or as required by applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

Taste Spin is not directed to children under the age of 13, or a higher minimum age where required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable age.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can review the matter.
