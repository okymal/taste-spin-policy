Account and Data Deletion — Taste Spin

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This page explains how users of Taste Spin – What to Eat can request deletion of their account and associated data, or request deletion of specific data without deleting their account.

Taste Spin uses Google Sign-In and Firebase services for account authentication and certain cloud features.

Your privacy matters. You can request account deletion or deletion of specific eligible data at any time, including after uninstalling the application.

1. Request Complete Account Deletion

If you have signed in to Taste Spin using your Google account and want to permanently delete your Taste Spin account and associated personal data, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to okymal111@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Taste Spin account".
  2. Whenever possible, send the request from the Google account used to sign in to Taste Spin. This helps us verify account ownership.
  3. If you no longer have access to that email account, contact us using another email address and explain the situation. We may request additional information necessary to verify ownership.
  4. After verifying your request, we will permanently delete your Taste Spin account and associated data under our control within 7 days, except information that must be retained for legitimate legal or security reasons.
  5. We will send a confirmation email when the deletion process has been completed.
Request Account Deletion

2. Request Data Deletion Without Deleting Your Account

You may also request deletion of specific personal or user-created data while keeping your Taste Spin account active.

Examples include eligible account-associated data stored in Firebase, such as user-created dish lists and saved custom food information.

  1. Send an email to okymal111@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Taste Spin data".
  2. Specify which information you want removed, such as a saved dish list or other account-associated information.
  3. Send the request from your registered Google account whenever possible.
  4. After verifying your request, we will delete the requested data under our control within 7 days, where technically possible and not subject to a legitimate retention requirement.
  5. Your Taste Spin account will remain active. We will confirm the outcome by email.
Request Data Deletion

3. What Data Is Deleted With Your Account

When a verified account deletion request is processed, we remove the following account-associated information under our control, where applicable:

  • Your Taste Spin authentication record stored in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your Google profile name, email address, and user identifiers stored as part of your Taste Spin account.
  • User-created dish lists and custom food information stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Other cloud-stored user information directly associated with your Taste Spin account.

Deleting your Taste Spin account does not delete your Google account or your personal Google profile.

Information stored only on your device may need to be removed separately by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the app.

4. Analytics and Technical Data

Taste Spin uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand application usage, improve stability, and investigate technical issues.

These services may process application activity, device identifiers, diagnostic information, crash reports, and approximate location derived from network information.

Account-associated information is removed from systems under our control where applicable. However, aggregated or de-identified statistical information, and certain technical records that cannot reasonably be linked to an individual account, may remain subject to the applicable service retention settings.

5. Taste Spin PLUS Subscriptions and Purchases

Taste Spin may offer an optional paid subscription called Taste Spin PLUS. Subscriptions and payments are processed through Google Play Billing.

Taste Spin does not receive or store your full payment card number or banking credentials. Purchase and subscription information may be processed to verify access to paid features and measure subscription performance.

Important: Deleting your Taste Spin account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription.

If you have an active Taste Spin PLUS subscription, please cancel it separately through Google Play to prevent future recurring charges:

  1. Open Google Play.
  2. Go to Payments & subscriptions.
  3. Select Subscriptions.
  4. Choose Taste Spin PLUS, if listed.
  5. Select Cancel subscription and follow the instructions.

You can also manage your subscriptions here:

Manage Google Play Subscriptions

You may submit an account deletion request even if your subscription has not yet been canceled. Account deletion does not automatically provide a refund.

6. What Data May Be Retained

After account deletion, we do not retain account-associated personal information unless retention is necessary for legitimate purposes, such as compliance with applicable laws, accounting requirements, fraud prevention, security, or resolving legal obligations.

Any such information is retained only for the period required by the applicable legal or security obligation.

Google Play may independently retain purchase, payment, subscription, and transaction records in accordance with Google's own privacy and retention policies.

Aggregated or de-identified analytics information may remain for the applicable analytics retention period where it is no longer associated with an identifiable Taste Spin account.

7. After Account Deletion

Once your Taste Spin account has been deleted, its associated cloud data cannot normally be recovered.

If you install Taste Spin again, you can sign in using your Google account and create a new Taste Spin profile.

The availability of any previously purchased subscription benefits will depend on the subscription status managed by Google Play.
